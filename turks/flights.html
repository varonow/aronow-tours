Good to know

  • Everyone needs a valid passport, babies included.
  • Turks and Caicos keeps the same time as New York.
  • Turks and Caicos uses US dollars.
  • The plugs and power are the same as at home. No adapters needed.
  • Providenciales airport is small, with two baggage carousels and limited WiFi. Luggage carts cost $5 and porters are on hand.
  • A driver from Mr. VIP meets every flight. You do not need to arrange a taxi.
  • Tag your bags with your room before you leave home. See Rooms.
