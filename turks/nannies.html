What the nannies have been told

  • Water safety comes first. They stay very close to the children in and around the pools, the beach and the dock.
  • This is a kosher home. All food for the children comes from the family kitchen. No outside food.
  • They check with the kitchen staff before using the villa kitchen.
  • The same nannies stay with the same children all week.
  • Kids eat with the nannies: breakfast from 7:00am, lunch around 12:00pm, dinner around 5:30pm.

All Little Angels nannies are background checked, trained in CPR and first aid, and can swim.
