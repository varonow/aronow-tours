The Maps

One journey · three cities · tap any pin

Around the world in four flights — three countries, no Saturday travel, home with the new year.

Everything starts at Gwanghwamun: the palace at your feet and Heesum ten minutes away, Bukchon just up the hill, Jamsil and Cheongdam across the river, and RAIM and the drums up north in Dobong and Nowon. A private driver has us all three days — tap a pin for the story.

The river is the highway — the hotel sits on it, the temple and flower market line it, and the canal boat crosses it. A guide and driver both full days.

Trisara on the quiet northwest coast, 20 minutes from the airport — and the gold dotted lines are the four boat days, all leaving from Ao Po Grand Marina: Phang Nga's caves, Racha's turquoise bays, the fishing morning off the Khai Islands, and the Phi Phi run with Monkey Beach.
