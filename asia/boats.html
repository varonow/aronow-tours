The Long-tail

A private Four Seasons long-tail into the Thonburi canals

The only boat of the trip that is not a yacht, and on purpose. A ruea hang yao is Bangkok's own boat: long, narrow and wooden, with a car engine on a pivot at the stern and a propeller at the end of a long shaft, so it can skim through canals far too shallow and narrow for anything else.

Yours is the hotel's private one, with cushioned seats and a canopy for shade. You step on at the Four Seasons pier, cross the Chao Phraya, and slip into the khlongs of Thonburi: stilt houses, tiny gardens, temple landings, the giant golden Buddha of Wat Paknam above the rooftops. It is low to the water and can be a little splashy when it picks up speed on the river, so the middle seats are the dry ones.

Phang Nga Bay · Mon Dec 28

Sambuca

53-foot Grand Banks motor yacht
Swipe for more photos · tap one to enlarge

A Grand Banks is the classic long-range cruiser: teak inside, a steady hull, and no hurry. At about 10 knots, Sambuca is the slowest boat of the week, and that is exactly right for Phang Nga, where the whole point is gliding between the limestone towers rather than racing past them.

The photos make it look like an indoor boat, but Julian says the flybridge is the real living room. It seats ten-plus in sun and shade, with more space on the aft deck, and there is a proper table where all four of you sit down together for lunch. The tender and two kayaks come along for the sea caves and hongs, and the captain times the route to the tides. Built in 2001 and fully refitted since, with two private cabins and two bathrooms, so changing into evening clothes for Café del Mar on the way back in is easy.

Racha Islands · Wed Dec 30

Scenery

58-foot Cranchi flybridge yacht
Swipe for more photos · tap one to enlarge

The splurge boat, and the one picked for the boat itself as much as the destination. Cranchi is an Italian yard, and the 58 is a sleek flybridge yacht: big, white, and built for long days at anchor with music on and everyone in and out of the water.

Julian's answer to "where do the four of us sit?" was everywhere: the aft deck, the flybridge and the bow are all shaded. Lunch is at the aft-deck table or in the air-conditioned salon if it is too hot. The flybridge has its own grill, fridge and icemaker, and the hydraulic swim platform lowers right to the water, with a floating yacht pool, a slide and paddleboards off the back. The captain has more than 25 years around Phuket and Krabi. It cruises at 16 to 17 knots, about an hour and 45 minutes to Racha.

Fishing · Khai Islands · Thu Dec 31

Ocean Pearl

42-foot sport cruiser
Swipe for more photos · tap one to enlarge

The fast one. Ocean Pearl is a 2024 sport cruiser with 600 horsepower and a top cruise around 30 knots, so very little of the morning goes to getting there. The Khai Islands are close, and the fishing grounds around them are where the crew takes you.

This is a fun-fishing morning, not a tournament. Julian confirmed rods for everyone and bait included, and the crew sets up and helps; they pick the method on the day and move the boat if the fish are not biting. The hardtop shades the lounge seating when you are not reeling, there is a bathroom on board, and a swim and snorkel stop at Khai before heading back to rest up for the gala.

Phi Phi Islands · Fri Jan 1

PRADA

50-foot power catamaran
Swipe for more photos · tap one to enlarge

The newest boat of the week, built in 2025, and the one whose layout matched the wish list most obviously: almost all of it is outdoor living space. Two hulls make a catamaran wide and steady, which matters on the longest crossing of the trip, about an hour and 45 minutes each way at 15 knots.

At water level there is a big shaded deck at the stern for lunch and the swim ladder; up top, a sun deck of pads under a shade sail; and between them an air-conditioned salon with a cabin and bathroom. Lunch is catered on board, ordered in advance: the vegan menu for four plus one whole fish, so no beach restaurant. The plan is the three must-dos, Pileh Lagoon, Monkey Beach and Maya Bay, then tell the crew when to head home, back at the marina by 3:30 to 4 for Cielo & Spice and Siam Niramit.
