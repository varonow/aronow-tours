10:00a Najeonchilgi workshop — mother-of-pearl, Bukchon ▾
🐚 CraftNajeonchilgi is the thousand-year-old royal Korean craft of mother-of-pearl inlay — slivers of iridescent shell set into deep black lacquer, the technique behind Korea's most treasured furniture and jewel boxes. A full three-hour session in a Bukchon atelier: the master's assistant leads your hands, the guide translates every step, and you each make a real piece. The lacquer needs time to set, so your finished pieces are delivered to the hotel that evening. For an interiors eye, the best three hours in Seoul.
🕙 10:00am–1:00pm  ·  📍 Bukchon Hanok Village  ·  pieces delivered to the Four Seasons that evening
