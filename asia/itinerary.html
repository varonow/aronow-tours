Land, Recover & Seoul After Dark
Monday, December 21, 2026
Gwanghwamun, Seoul
Morning Land at Incheon — VIP arrival service ▾
🛬 VIP arrivalWheels down at 6:30am after the long one — and a VIP airport team is waiting to walk all four of you through immigration and baggage. No lines, no thinking. Then a private car sweeps you the ~45 minutes into Seoul. Bags to the bell desk, and somewhere to freshen up on arrival while the rooms are readied. The trip has begun; the sightseeing can wait.
📍 Four Seasons Hotel Seoul — 97 Saemunan-ro, Jongno-gu  ·  ☎ +82 2-6388-5000  ·  Aronow conf. 4377284930 · Salame conf. 4377333669
✓ VIP airport service booked — met on landing, escorted through arrivals
8:30a Breakfast & coffee at the hotel, freshen up ▾
🍳 BreakfastNo alarm-clock tourism after sixteen hours in the air. A slow breakfast and good coffee downstairs at The Market Kitchen, the hotel's bright market-style dining room, then freshen up and change before heading out. Ease into the day.
⚡ The concierge is arranging a place to freshen up on arrival, since the rooms may not be ready this early.
11:00a Driver & guide → Lotte World Tower for the afternoon ▾
🚗 OutA private car and English-speaking guide take you across the river to Lotte World Tower — Korea's tallest building and a whole afternoon in one place: a spa, dining with a view, the Seoul Sky observation decks, and a luxury mall. The perfect low-stress first outing, and daylight and gentle movement are the best cure for the jet lag.
⚡ Arrange — car + English-speaking guide for the day (follow-up to the concierge).
11:30a Signiel Spa — at Lotte World Tower ▾
💆 SpaThe spa at Signiel, high in the tower — one of Seoul's most special, more of an occasion than the hotel spa. Massages and restorative treatments to send the whole flight down the drain, and a place to freshen up properly before the rest of the afternoon. A glorious reset with a view.
📍 Signiel Spa — Lotte World Tower  ·  🔗 lottehotel.com/signiel-seoul
⚡ Book directly — reserve treatments for all four (booking ourselves, not via the Four Seasons).
1:30p Lunch at the tower ▾
🍽 LunchA relaxed lunch with a view at The Lounge on the 79th floor of Signiel — a restaurant and bar, lighter and more casual than the fine-dining room, and just what you want after the flight (Akira Back is dinner). Right there in the tower, an easy step from the spa.
⚡ Book when December opens — The Lounge takes reservations; December isn't open yet. Ask on the spa call. (STAY, the 81st-floor Yannick Alléno French room, is the fine-dining alternative if ever wanted.)
3:00p Seoul Sky — floors 117–123 ▾
🌆 Seoul SkyAt 555 meters, glass floors and a 360° sweep of a ten-million-person city spilling into the mountains. Warm, indoors, and the fastest way to grasp just how big Seoul is — and a short ride up from where you already are.
📍 Lotte World Tower — 300 Olympic-ro, Songpa-gu  ·  🔗 seoulsky.lotteworld.com
⚡ ₩29,000pp. Book online to skip the line.
4:00p Back to the hotel — into the rooms, rest ▾
The driver brings you back across the river. Into your rooms by now, unpack, and a short rest before the evening — a little shopping in the Lotte mall first only if anyone still has the energy.
5:00p Heesum — Hands-On Korean Tea Ceremony ▾
🍵 TeaFive minutes away, a certified tea master walks the four of you through the Korean darye — the gestures, the meaning, two premium teas from Korea's finest regions, poured by your own hands. Seventy minutes of dedicated instruction and tasting: calm, meditative, the best possible antidote to a red-eye.
📍 Heesum Tea Room — Gwanghwamun, 5–10 min from the hotel  ·  🔗 heesum.com
✓ Booked — with Jason  ·  5:00pm  ·  ₩160,000 per guest, settled on-site  ·  70 minutes
7:15p Akira Back — dinner in the hotel ▾
🍽 DinnerThe easiest possible arrival-night dinner: straight back from the tea and downstairs to Akira Back, the hotel's own modern Japanese room from the Michelin-distinguished chef. A dramatic, double-height dining hall, stylish but warm — and a menu made for the group: sashimi, grilled fish, the famous tuna pizza, tempura and vegetable dishes, plus vegan options. No cab, no navigating, just a beautiful dinner and the elevator to bed.
📍 Akira Back — Four Seasons Hotel Seoul (in-house)  ·  Dinner 5:30–10pm (last order 9:30)  ·  Smart casual — long pants + closed-toe shoes for the gentlemen  ·  🔗 fourseasons.com/seoul
⚡ Book — dinner for 4; note no meat/poultry/shellfish + one gluten-free (the kitchen handles this easily).
✓ Confirmed — Dec 21, 7:15pm · 4 guests · 12th-floor general seating · dietary noted with the kitchen · (festive menu/pricing TBC)
9:30p Optional nightcap — Charles H. back at the hotel ▾
🍸 NightcapEntirely optional, and all under one roof: either straight up to bed after a long first day, or a few steps to Charles H. — one of Asia's most celebrated cocktail bars, hidden in your hotel's basement. Soju-based cocktails you won't find at home and no cab to catch. However the night goes, you're already home.

🍱 LunchA good fish-and-vegetable Korean lunch near the shops — let the concierge pick the room.
2:30p Shopping — Cheongdam + Apgujeong with an insider ▾
🛍 ShoppingNot Gucci and Prada. A private local shopper takes you to Korean designers the rest of the world is copying — Gentle Monster (each store a full art installation, sometimes with roaming robots), Ader Error's designed flagship, and finds you simply cannot get in New York. Men included — no sofa duty.

5:00p The afternoon fork — sports vs K-beauty ▾
⛳ SplitMarshall & Ted, Gangnam: a private bay at Golfzon (play Pebble or Augusta on the best sim tech on earth, food and drinks in the bay), then Sports Monster — VR baseball against real pitchers, archery, a dunk zone, 30+ ways to compete indoors. Victoria & Susan, Apgujeong: a private K-beauty guide who knows what's actually worth it for Western skin — the Sulwhasoo flagship, Olive Young, the best boutiques, no rushing.

8:30p Dinner — Schedule Cheongdam ▾
🍽 DinnerDinner right in the neighborhood you spent the afternoon shopping — Schedule Cheongdam is one of the most stylish rooms in Gangnam, all European-inspired design and a fashionable crowd, with a menu that slides happily between Western plates and Korean comfort. Trendy without trying too hard, and open late if the night runs long.
📍 Schedule Cheongdam — 37 Seolleung-ro 152-gil, Gangnam (9 min from Apgujeong Rodeo)  ·  ☎ +82 2-2548-0236  ·  🔗 schedulecorp.co.kr
10:30p Once in a Blue Moon — Seoul's finest jazz ▾
🎷 Live jazzGangnam's celebrated jazz room — world-class players, great cocktails, a sophisticated night out rather than a serious concert. The right finish to the tech-and-shopping day.

The Really Korean Day
Wednesday, December 23, 2026
Seoul & Suwon
9:30a Breakfast — Tartine or Passion 5 ▾
🥐 BreakfastEither the San Francisco sourdough icon transplanted to Seoul, or a patisserie that rivals Paris. A very good start to the last Seoul day.
10:30a Kimchi master class — the luxury version ▾
🥬 KimchiNot the tourist one. A private session with a master maker in a traditional kitchen — the fermentation science, the regional variations, the history, and your own batch to take home. Hands-on, genuinely Korean, and perfect for the group's diet.
⚡ Book — ask the concierge for a private luxury session, not a group class.
12:30p Lunch near the kimchi kitchen ▾
🍜 LunchSomething easy before drumming.
1:30p Korean drumming — private Samulnori session ▾
🥁 DrummingA private instructor at the National Gugak Center teaches all four of you traditional percussion — the hourglass Janggu and the four-instrument ensemble — building to the four of you playing together. Loud, physical, and the story you'll tell for years.
📍 National Gugak Center — 2364 Nambusunhwan-ro, Seocho-gu  ·  🔗 gugak.go.kr
⚡ Book — 90-minute private session via the concierge or National Gugak Center.
3:45p Samsung Innovation Museum — Suwon, private tour ▾
📱 SamsungA 45-minute drive to Samsung's own HQ. Five floors from the first transistors to 6G and semiconductors — genuinely fascinating for people who run a telecom company. Book the English guided tour and let the guide go deep.
📍 Samsung Innovation Museum — 129 Samsung-ro, Yeongtong-gu, Suwon  ·  🔗 samsunginnovationmuseum.com
⚡ Book — weekday visits need advance reservation; request an English guide. Free entry, ~2 hrs.
8:15p Final Seoul dinner — People the Terrace ▾
🌟 Big dinnerThe plan for the last Seoul night: Korea's largest dining bar, and one of Cheongdam's most glamorous rooms — contemporary fusion plates, a serious cocktail and wine program, and the kind of crowd where you might spot a familiar face from a K-drama. Dinner that turns into an evening rather than ending at dessert.
📍 People the Terrace — 13 Dosan-daero 81-gil, Cheongdam, Gangnam  ·  Open 6pm–2am
⚡ Current thinking — not booked yet. Reserve ahead; it's a scene.
10:15p Final Seoul night — live Western music ▾
🎸 Live musicA great band playing songs you actually know — classic rock, 70s/80s, soul, somewhere in Hongdae or Itaewon. Or the sophisticated 1914 Lounge at Josun Palace for late cocktails.

9:30a Phuket Mountain Elephant Care — hands-on in the jungle ▾
