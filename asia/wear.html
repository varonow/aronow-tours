Packing, Solved

What to Wear

One suitcase · two seasons · four boats · one gala
The suitcase math. Two seasons, one suitcase — solved: pack Seoul's winter layer as ONE warm system (coat, gloves, hat) and we ship the winter clothes home from Seoul before flying to Bangkok. From Dec 24 on, it's tropics only.
🧥
Seoul
Dec 21 – 24 · dress warm, dress well
🧥One real winter coat plus gloves, hat and a scarf — it's in the 20s at night, and it all ships home Dec 24.
👕Layers — sweaters over shirts; every interior is warmly heated, so you'll be peeling.
👞Comfortable shoes with grip — short walks between cars and doors, possibly on a dusting of snow.
👗Two dressier evening looks — Schedule Cheongdam and People the Terrace are stylish rooms; Seoul dresses up.
👘Hanbok day (Dec 22) — they dress you in silk for the portraits; wear simple base layers that morning.
🧖Spa day (Day 1) — the hotel provides everything; arrive in whatever's comfortable off the plane.
🌴
Bangkok
Dec 24 – 27 · light, breathable, one smart night
👕Lightest fabrics you own — linen and cotton; upper 80s and sunny every day.
🛕Temple morning (Dec 25) — shoulders and knees covered at Wat Arun; a light shawl works.
👞Real walking shoes — Chatuchak and Chinatown are miles on your feet; sandals for the rest.
🍸Sirocco night (Dec 24) — smart dress enforced on the rooftop: no shorts, no sandals for the men.
🧢Hat and sunglasses — the flower-market-to-Chinatown tuk-tuk night is warm; day sun is strong.
🐚
Phuket
Dec 27 – Jan 3 · resort life + one gala
🏊More swimwear than you think — villa pool plus four boat days; suits never quite dry by morning.
⛵Boat-day kit — cover-up, non-slip footing (barefoot works aboard), sun shirt, hat that ties, dry bag for phones.
🧴Serious sunscreen — reef-safe for the lagoons; the Andaman sun is no joke even in 'winter.'
👗Beach-club evenings — Catch and Café del Mar are chic-casual: linen, flowy, barefoot-adjacent.
💎New Year's Eve — 'Jewel Elegance in the Garden' — THE dress-up night of the trip. Bring the good jewelry and something spectacular; the men in elegant tropical formal.
🐘Elephant morning (Dec 29) — clothes that can get muddy and wet, water shoes, and a full change for after.
